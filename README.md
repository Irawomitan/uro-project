Uro Documentation Project

Uro is an endangered Akokoid language spoken in Ajowa-Akoko, Ondo State, Nigeria. This repository hosts the official website of the Uro Documentation Project — a community-driven effort to record, preserve, and share the Uro language.

The project was carried out by 28 undergraduate students from the Department of Linguistics and African Languages, Obafemi Awolowo University, Ile-Ife. With no institutional funding, each member contributed ₦10,000 from their own pocket — a total of ₦280,000, with an additional ₦40,000 in external contributions. The Departmental Phonetics Laboratory kindly lent recording equipment for fieldwork.

Working directly with the Uro-speaking community in Ajowa-Akoko, the team documented over 1,200 words, elicited sentences and oral narratives, recorded songs, folklore, and everyday speech, and produced a bilingual dictionary, thematic flashcard sets, community storybooks, and language games. Sociolinguistic data and annotated audio/video recordings are in progress.
We call ourselves Stronghead Linguists. We think that says everything.
